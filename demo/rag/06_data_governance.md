# Data governance and report definitions — NexaVale Labs

SYNTHETIC DEMO ONLY: No real customer or financial data is present.

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer personal data and payroll are Restricted. Internal process guides are Internal unless they contain secrets. Passwords, API keys and private keys must never be uploaded into the knowledge base. The knowledge base is shared across authorized users and is not a separate document-permission system.

In Orion, net revenue means invoiced value excluding VAT, minus credit notes. Draft and cancelled invoices are excluded. An overdue invoice has an unpaid balance and a due date earlier than the report date. A late payment received yesterday is not an unpaid overdue invoice today.

Monthly figures close on the fifth business day of the following month. Finance Operations owns the definition of revenue. The retention period for the fictional invoice archive is seven years. Questions about deletions must be escalated to the data owner; a chatbot answer is not authorization to delete records.
