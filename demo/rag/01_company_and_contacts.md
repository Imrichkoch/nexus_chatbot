# NexaVale Labs — company and contacts

SYNTHETIC DEMO: This company, all people, amounts and procedures are fictional. Not a real operational policy.

NexaVale Labs is a fictional software company with 84 employees. Its headquarters are in Bratislava; a second office is in Brno. Office hours are 09:00–17:00 CET/CEST on working days. The internal knowledge owner is Martina Vargová, Operations Lead.

Employees contact the Service Desk at helpdesk@nexavale.example. The Service Desk acknowledges ordinary requests within four business hours. Access requests need approval from the employee's manager. Security incidents must go to security@nexavale.example immediately, not to the ordinary request queue.

The Atlas customer portal is owned by the Product team. The Orion reporting service is owned by Finance Operations. This distinction identifies the business owner, not the server administrator.
