# Expenses and purchasing policy — NexaVale Labs

SYNTHETIC DEMO ONLY: All limits, names and examples are fictional.

An employee submits an expense claim within 30 calendar days of the purchase. A receipt, business reason and cost centre are mandatory. Claims up to EUR 250 need line-manager approval. Claims above EUR 250 and up to EUR 2,000 additionally need Finance approval. Purchases above EUR 2,000 require Finance approval and two comparable vendor quotes before ordering. These thresholds exclude VAT.

The standard hotel limit is EUR 140 per night excluding city tax. A higher rate needs written manager approval before booking. Personal minibar purchases are never reimbursed. Rail travel is reimbursed in second class; air travel requires manager approval before booking.

Invoices are payable 30 days after receipt of a correct invoice. A missing purchase-order number blocks payment until Procurement resolves it. Procurement owner: Daniel Král, procurement@nexavale.example.
